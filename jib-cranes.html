JIB Cranes

Localised workstation lifting where overhead cranes aren't practical.

Built for Workstation Lifting

Our JIB cranes provide targeted lifting capability exactly where it's needed, making them perfect for assembly stations, loading bays, and machining cells where a full overhead system isn't required.

Available in both wall-mounted and freestanding configurations with 180° to 360° rotation, these cranes integrate seamlessly with existing overhead systems or operate as standalone workstations.

Technical Specifications

Capacity 0.5T – 10T
Rotation 180° – 360°
Reach Up to 6m
Duty Class FEM 1Am – 2m
Power Supply 230–480V, 50/60Hz

Engineered for Precision Workstation Lifting

Whether mounted to a structural column or freestanding, our JIB cranes deliver smooth, ergonomic load handling exactly where your operators need it most. The robust slew ring design ensures effortless rotation, even under maximum capacity.
