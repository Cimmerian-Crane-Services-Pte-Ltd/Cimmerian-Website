About Cimmerian

Three decades of engineering excellence, unwavering commitment to safety, and a relentless drive to innovate in the crane services and heavy material handling sector.

Our Heritage

Cimmerian Crane Services is a premier provider of heavy industrial material handling solutions, recognized for our deep technical expertise and commitment to operational excellence. As the sole authorized distributor for ABUS in the region, we bridge the gap between world-class German engineering and the precise demands of the Southeast Asian market.

For over three decades, we have partnered with manufacturing, marine, and logistics facilities to deliver lifting ecosystems that maximize safety, uptime, and productivity. We do not just supply equipment; we engineer comprehensive, compliant, and future-proof lifting solutions tailored to your facility's unique operational challenges.
