Gantry Cranes

Heavy-duty outdoor and indoor crane systems engineered for demanding environments.

Built for Heavy-Duty Lifting

Our gantry cranes are engineered for outdoor and heavy industrial environments where overhead travelling cranes are not practical. These freestanding systems provide reliable material handling solutions for yards, shipyards, container terminals, and construction sites.

Available in various configurations including full gantry, semi-gantry, and portable designs, each system is custom-engineered to meet your specific span, height, and capacity requirements with robust structural integrity.

Technical Specifications

Capacity 5T – 1000T+
Span Up to 50m
Lift Height Up to 40m
Duty Class FEM 1Am – 5m
Configuration Single/Double Girder

Uncompromising Strength for Outdoor Environments

Our gantry cranes are built to withstand the harshest outdoor conditions. With IP65-rated components, marine-grade coatings, and robust box-girder designs, they deliver reliable, high-capacity lifting in shipyards, container terminals, and heavy construction sites.
