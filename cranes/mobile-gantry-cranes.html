Lightweight Mobile Gantry Cranes

Portable, fast-assembly gantry cranes for flexible operations.

Built for Flexible Operations

Our lightweight mobile gantry cranes are designed for rapid deployment in environments where fixed crane infrastructure isn't practical, offering tool-free assembly and adjustable configurations.

Perfect for maintenance tasks, small-scale fabrication, and temporary lifting needs, these cranes deliver reliable performance in both indoor and outdoor environments with minimal setup time.

Technical Specifications

Capacity 0.5T – 10T
Span Adjustable up to 12m
Height Adjustable up to 8m
Duty Class FEM 1Am – 2m
Power Supply 230V / 480V, 50/60Hz

Rapid Deployment, Zero Compromise

When fixed infrastructure isn't an option, our lightweight mobile gantries provide immediate, reliable lifting power. With tool-free assembly and adjustable heights, they adapt to your changing workshop or field requirements in minutes, not hours.
