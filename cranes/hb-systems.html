HB-Systems

Hybrid bridge systems optimised for low headroom environments.

Built for Low Headroom Spaces

HB-Systems combine the strength of overhead travelling cranes with a compact, space-saving design that maximises vertical clearance in facilities with limited headroom.

These hybrid bridge systems deliver high duty cycle performance while maintaining a minimal footprint, making them ideal for retrofit projects and modern compact facilities.

Technical Specifications

Capacity 2T – 50T
Span Up to 25m
Lift Height Custom (low headroom)
Duty Class FEM 1Am – 3m
Power Supply 380–480V, 50/60Hz

Maximum Lifting, Minimal Footprint

The HB-System hybrid bridge crane is the ultimate solution for facilities with severe height restrictions. By optimizing the hoist and trolley design, we reclaim up to 30% of vertical space compared to standard overhead cranes, making it ideal for retrofit projects and compact manufacturing cells.
