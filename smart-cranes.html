ABUControl: Intelligence Built into Every Movement

ABUControl is ABUS's flagship smart crane control system — engineered to elevate operator experience, load precision, and maintenance intelligence into one unified platform. Functions are tuned to your specific area of application, with intuitive ease of use and a modern interface for real-time monitoring and configuration.

Built on open, industry-standard components from renowned electronics manufacturers, ABUControl gives you full ownership of your crane — no proprietary lock-in, no specialist-only licenses. Service and repair remain entirely in your hands.

01

Precision & Safety

Anti-Sway Control

The ABUS sway control system increases safety and convenience during the transport of goods in sensitive areas. Powered by real-time mathematical calculations that factor in travel speeds, acceleration and deceleration of both crane and hoist, hook position, and the length of the load lifting attachment.

Even operators who rarely use cranes can handle loads safely and precisely thanks to automatic sway dampening on every axis.

02

Multi-Hoist Coordination

Synchronisation Control

The ABUS synchronisation system enables safe handling of long products with two hoists mounted on a single crane — divergence of lifting speeds is prevented through constant closed-loop regulation, even when different wire rope hoists are used simultaneously.

The system scales to two cranes with a maximum of four hoists operating in tandem travel motions, all coordinated and regulated from a single ABUControl unit.

03

Adaptive Motion

Travel & Hoist Profiles

ABUControl-equipped cranes adapt to your application through configurable travel and hoist profiles. A crane running two fixed speeds behaves entirely differently from one driven by a frequency converter — and profiles let operators switch between them instantly.

Operators no longer need to manually adjust existing cranes for each task. Pre-configured profiles increase productivity and make the transition to a new crane system seamless for every team.
